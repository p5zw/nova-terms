Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Information We Process

NOVA Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, display names, command inputs, and permission information when needed to operate its features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to commands, configure server channels and roles, operate bot features, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse.

3. Data Storage

Information may be processed temporarily while the bot operates. Persistent storage depends on the bot's code and hosting configuration. The operator should verify actual storage and deletion practices before making additional claims.

4. Sharing

NOVA does not intentionally sell users' personal information. Discord and the bot's hosting provider may process information as necessary to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Discord Privacy Policy

5. Permissions

Server owners and administrators control the bot's permissions. Only authorized administrators should use commands that create or modify channels and roles.

6. Your Choices

You can stop using NOVA or ask a server administrator to remove it. Contact the bot owner through the community where NOVA is used with privacy questions or requests concerning information the operator can access.

7. Security

The operator should take reasonable steps to protect bot credentials and stored information. Never share the bot token publicly. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

8. Changes

This policy may change when the bot's features or data practices change. The published version should accurately reflect the bot's actual behavior.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the NOVA Bot owner through the Discord community where the bot is used.

NOVA is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Discord.
